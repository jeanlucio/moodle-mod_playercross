# ✨ Features

* 🧩 **Deduction Crossword Gameplay:** Each round picks a **mystery phrase** (a course concept's own clue) and a set of **terms** (other concepts whose words share letters with the mystery phrase). Solving a term reveals its shared letters everywhere they occur — in every pending term and in the mystery phrase itself — by letter identity, not by spatial position.
* 🎯 **Direct Mystery-Phrase Guess:** Students can risk a direct guess of the mystery phrase at any point in the round, without waiting to resolve every term first. Guessing correctly before resolving any term earns an early-guess bonus — see [Grading & Ranking](#grading).
* ⚖️ **Configurable Win Condition:** Choose whether a round is won only by resolving every term **and** guessing the mystery phrase, or by the mystery-phrase guess alone (terms still help by revealing letters, but are optional to finish the round). See [Grading & Ranking](#grading).
* 🛡️ **Automatic Loss on Term Exhaustion:** Under the "both required" win condition, a term that runs out of attempts makes winning mathematically impossible — the round ends immediately as a loss instead of leaving the student stuck.
* 🚫 **Automatic Loss on Mystery-Phrase Exhaustion:** The mystery phrase has its own configurable attempt limit. Running out always ends the round as a loss, regardless of win condition — the phrase is required to win either way.
* 🔡 **Uncovered-Slot Handling (Configurable):** A mystery-phrase letter that no selected term happens to share is, by default, revealed automatically from the start of the round (no term could ever reveal it "for free" otherwise); teachers can disable this so it stays hidden until a paid hint or the round's end.
* 📖 **Glossary Integration:** Import concepts from one or all course glossaries as the word pool, with definitions used as each word's clue.
* ✂️ **Split Multi-Word Concepts (optional, off by default):** A multi-word glossary concept (e.g. "Operating System") can be split into separate single-word candidates. Off by default to favour clue quality — when disabled, that concept is simply left out of the pool instead of producing isolated terms that do not always make sense on their own.
* 🚧 **Configurable Stopwords:** A per-activity, comma-separated list of words (e.g. "the, of, and") to ignore when splitting a multi-word glossary concept into candidates — only relevant while splitting concepts above is enabled.
* 🤖 **AI Word Generation (Optional):** Generate candidate words and clues for a given topic via [AI Hub](#aihub) (`local_aihub`) or Moodle's `core_ai` fallback. Generated words are treated as untrusted input — only single-token, purely alphabetic terms within the configured length bounds are saved, and they enter the pool pending teacher approval.
* ✍️ **Manual Word Pool:** Teachers can add, edit, approve, and delete words directly from the management page.
* 🚫 **Duplicate Prevention:** The same word text can only exist once per activity pool, regardless of which source added it — a manual entry blocks a colliding glossary import (and vice versa), so random selection is never skewed toward one accidentally-duplicated word.
* 🔤 **Manual-Word Validation:** A manually added word must be letters only, within the configured length range, and not already in the pool; the form rejects an invalid one with a specific reason instead of silently accepting a word the game could never actually draw.
* 🧩 **Fragmented-Concept Warning:** A multi-word glossary concept split into several single-word entries is flagged in the word management table, since each sibling word still carries the full original concept's definition as its clue.
* 🔍 **Live Eligible-Words Count:** The settings form shows, live via AJAX, how many words currently fall within the configured length range — checked separately for the mystery phrase's own range and the terms' range — against the real pool while editing an existing activity, or a preview of the selected glossary's content while creating a brand-new one.
* 🔍 **Inactive Word Detection:** Pool words that can no longer be drawn (wrong length for their role, invalid characters, still pending approval) are flagged by name, with the reason, in the word management table; an active/inactive word count is shown above the game area for whoever can manage the activity.
* 🎯 **Draw-Count Tracking:** The word management table shows how many times each word has actually been drawn as the mystery phrase, counted live from attempt history — no recompute step needed.
* 🔀 **Word Modes:** Random mystery phrase per round (default) or shared sequence mode, where every student receives the same puzzle in the same order — terms are never drawn separately, so the same phrase always brings the same terms.
* 📏 **Independent Length Ranges:** The mystery phrase's minimum (and optional maximum) letter-count is configured separately from the term words' own length range, so a long, descriptive clue can still produce a manageable puzzle.
* 🔢 **Configurable Term Count:** 3 to 10 terms per round (default 5), balancing puzzle difficulty against pool size.
* ⏳ **Configurable Attempts, Per Term and For the Mystery Phrase:** Cap the number of guesses allowed per individual term, and separately for the mystery phrase itself (0 = unlimited for either; both default to 3). Choosing Linear scoring for the grade or the ranking requires both to be a real number.
* 💡 **Hint System (Toggleable):** A single round-wide "Hint" button reveals one letter at a time — that letter shows up in every pending term and the mystery phrase that share it, just like solving a term would — optionally at an item cost via PlayerHUD. Teachers can turn the reveal-hint mechanic off entirely for the activity; when left on (the default), it can be capped to a fixed number of reveals per round (default 3) or left unlimited. Revealing enough hints to uncover every letter finishes and wins the round on its own, without needing every term resolved.
* 🏳️ **Give Up:** Students can forfeit the current round at any time — the correct mystery phrase and every term word are revealed immediately.
* ⏱️ **Configurable Cooldown:** Minimum wait between rounds (minutes, hours, or days), always recomputed from the activity's current setting.
* 🔢 **Round Limit:** Teachers can cap the total number of rounds per student (1–10 or unlimited).
* 🛡️ **Round-Limit Integrity:** A round abandoned mid-play (closed tab, lost session) still counts against the round limit — reserved the moment it starts, not only once it finishes, so it can never grant a free re-roll.
* 🔡 **Accent-Insensitive Matching:** Diacritics are always stripped before comparing a guess and its target.
* ⌨️ **Accent Picker on the Virtual Keyboard:** Long-pressing a key with accented variants (most vowels, plus C, N and S) on the on-screen keyboard opens a popup with those variants, mirroring a mobile keyboard's own long-press behaviour — sliding the finger picks a variant, releasing confirms it. Covers Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian and German diacritics (including Ç, Ñ and ß); alphabets outside that set (Nordic, Slavic, Turkish, Icelandic...) are a possible future addition, not covered today. Purely cosmetic: guess matching stays accent-insensitive throughout.
* ⌨️ **Keyboard Marks Already-Revealed Letters:** A letter already revealed in any pending term or in the mystery phrase is highlighted on the virtual keyboard, so the student doesn't waste time retrying something already known.
* 🟢 **Submit-Ready Cue:** A term's row (or the mystery phrase's own row) reveals its own submit button the instant every one of its boxes is filled, right next to the tiles — no need to fill in every other term first, and no separate button to look for elsewhere on screen. A physical Enter key works too; the on-screen keyboard itself carries only letters and Backspace, matching the original game this plugin is based on.
* ✅ **True-Spelling Reveal:** When a term is resolved, the mystery phrase is guessed correctly, or the round ends, the term word and the mystery phrase are shown with their real, accented spelling from the word bank, even if the winning guess was typed without accents. A term left entirely spelled out by already-shared letters auto-resolves the moment the mystery phrase is guessed correctly, instead of staying marked unresolved.
* 🧮 **Binary/Linear Scoring:** Grade and ranking each choose their own scoring mode independently — Binary (full credit on a win) or Linear (credit decreases with every wrong guess, across every term and the mystery phrase together) — plus an early-guess bonus for guessing the mystery phrase directly before any term is resolved — see [Grading & Ranking](#grading).
* 📊 **Grading Methods:** Highest grade, average grade, first attempt, last attempt, or average over all required rounds.
* 🧮 **Grading Transparency:** Students see the active grading method before playing, the same way mod_quiz communicates its own grading method.
* 📋 **Gradebook Integration:** Grades are written automatically on every round completion.
* ✅ **Custom Completion Rule:** Minimum number of completed rounds, evaluated and applied immediately after each round.
* 🔄 **Course Reset Support:** "Reset course" clears student attempts and resets grades for the activity, scoped to the target course only.
* 🏆 **Top 5 Ranking:** Leaderboard scoped to the activity, capped to the top 5, with an outsider row so a lower-ranked student still sees their own real position. Respects `SEPARATEGROUPS`.
* 📋 **Attempt History:** Students review every finished round of their own — mystery phrase, terms resolved, attempts used, time, and score — via the toolbar. Whoever can manage the activity sees that same page too (including their own attempts, if any), plus a separate all-students report — see [Grading & Ranking](#grading).
* 🗑️ **Attempt Deletion:** From the all-students report, whoever can manage the activity can delete attempts individually or in bulk (with a select-the-current-page option). The affected student's grade is recalculated automatically, including unlocking activity settings that lock once a real grade exists, if every one of their attempts is removed. Already-granted PlayerHUD items are not reverted automatically — an on-page notice covers this whenever the activity grants items for winning.
* ❓ **In-Game Help:** A dedicated help page explains the puzzle mechanic, how to submit a guess, attempts, hints, timer, the activity's win condition, grading method and (when Linear scoring is active) the scoring formula — plus, when the relevant attempt limits are set, the risk of losing the round if a term's or the mystery phrase's attempts run out.
* 👋 **First-Visit Onboarding:** The how-to-play modal opens automatically the very first time a user visits any PlayerCross activity on the site — once, ever, site-wide — and never repeats after that; the toolbar help icon always reopens it on demand.
* ♿ **Accessibility:** WCAG AA contrast on every puzzle tile; non-colour state indicators; `aria-label` on every input; per-letter-box focus behaviour mirrors a verification-code input for predictable keyboard and click navigation.
* ⚡ **AJAX-Powered:** Every round transition (term guess, final guess, hint, forfeit, timeout, start, new round) happens without a page reload.
* 🔔 **Toast Feedback:** Round-flow outcomes (a term resolved, a wrong guess, a hint revealed) surface as brief, auto-dismissing toast notifications instead of blocking dialogs, keeping the student in the puzzle.
* 🎮 **PlayerHUD Integration (Optional):** Require inventory items to start a round or to reveal a hint, with atomic FIFO consumption. The student's current balance against the required quantity is always shown up front, and a cost pointing at a deleted or another course's item is waived rather than blocking the student. Can also **grant** an item for each round won; matching PlayerHUD's own anti-farming rule, no XP is awarded from that item while the activity allows unlimited rounds, and the potential win-grant XP is reflected in PlayerHUD's own "Total XP in the game" ceiling estimate.
* 🛡️ **Safe Cross-Course Integration:** Every PlayerHUD item reference is validated against the course's own block instance, never a stale or another course's item — even after backup/restore or course duplication.
* 🔔 **PlayerHUD Dependency Awareness:** When PlayerHUD is installed on the site but hasn't been added to the current course yet, the settings form explains that the integration will appear once the block is added. When the block_playerhud plugin isn't installed anywhere on the site, the site administrator sees an equivalent notice on the plugin's own settings page.
* 📦 **Backup & Restore:** Full Moodle 2 backup/restore support, including the "Duplicate activity" action, word pool, attempts, user/glossary id remapping, and safe PlayerHUD item remapping.
* 🔐 **Privacy API:** GDPR/LGPD compliant — complete data export and deletion for all stored personal data.
